# MUSE AUDIT — Issue #4: deployed-vs-candidate divergence + verdict validity
**Date:** 2026-10-19 (~01:25 EDT) · **Auditor:** Muse · **Subject build:** production `4423a07` · **Evidence:** probe measurements + screenshots in `~/workspace/blindrise-audit/issue4-probe/` (390×844 portrait / 844×390 landscape, 9-handed and 2-handed, production fonts)

## 1. Divergence: NONE
Production serves `4423a07` byte-for-byte (served app.js SHA-256 `c056b1cb…`, re-verified after the tournament; `/healthz` ok, epoch 2, dirty 0). The candidate Muse audited, the build the owner accepted on his iPhone, and the build now live are the same bytes. **The owner's 23:32 screenshot shows the shipped design itself — not a deployment mismatch, not a regression, and not an unshipped redesign.** Any explanation of the screenshot must be found in the design, and in what the audits measured.

## 2. The owner's six complaints, reproduced and measured (390×844 portrait, 9-handed)
1. **"Tall/narrow oval" — REPRODUCED.** The portrait felt is 337×402 px, aspect h/w **1.194** — and that is inside the suite's accepted range: `t_table_layout` allows portrait h/w up to **1.42** (FELT_MAX), with a floor of 0.95, relaxed to 0.8 on short screens. The test's own comment calls this "only a little taller than wide". Claude's root-cause note on Issue #3 (redesign set the portrait felt 1.2–1.3× taller than wide) matches the measurement. The shape the owner rejects is the shape the criteria accept.
2. **"Excessive unused lower viewport" — REPRODUCED, with a precise anatomy.** The felt ends at 67.1% of viewport height; **278 px (32.9%) of the screen sits below it**. Most of that zone is the dock/action controls (212 px) — functional, not empty. The pure waste is an unassigned band between the hero seat's bottom and the table-area edge (~31–35 px mid-hand; 51 px in the 2-handed layout), plus the felt floating with 19.4% of the screen above it. On the owner's phone, with the hero folded between hands, the whole below-felt region reads as one large dark void (see probe screenshot: table ends ~2/3 down, dock collapsed to a single status line). Landscape has no such problem (felt aspect 0.478, 87.1% height used, no empty band >20 px).
3. **"Obstructive Reset overlay" — PARTIALLY REPRODUCED (coach tip measured; Reset not directly observed).** The coach tip renders 370×96 px across the table's lower area, covering 5,734 px² of the hero seat and 479 px² of the hero's cards, its bottom edge 4 px from the table-area bottom — a large, non-trivial obstruction of the player's own seat while a hand is live. The Reset overlay specifically was not triggered during the probe; its obstruction is accepted from the owner's screenshot and remains to be measured on Claude's candidate.
4. **"Clipped winner text" — REPRODUCED, root-caused.** The winner line is hard-clamped to one line: style.css line 1741 applies `-webkit-line-clamp: 1; overflow: hidden` to `.msg`. Measured on every showdown variant (152 frames): the element needs 32 px (two lines) and gets 16 px (one) — **the second line is cut on 100% of results**, e.g. "Evangeline wins 54,225 · Three of a Kind, Aces" renders as one clipped line. The duplicate info line at the screen bottom is legible, so the outcome is readable — but the on-table winner presentation is clipped by construction, exactly as the owner reported.
5. **"Seat/nameplate/card positioning" — MEASURED, no defect of the old class.** All 9 seats sit inside the table area with centers on the felt; zero card-vs-plate/avatar overlaps (the 443a27f P1 stays fixed). What remains is the aesthetic consequence of the tall oval: seats hug the felt rim with boxes extending past it, and the hero plate sits at the felt's bottom edge.
6. **"Unclear end-of-hand state" — PARTIALLY REPRODUCED.** At the result the pot readout disappears (empty, invisible) and the table's own result line is the clipped element of item 4; the clear statement of the outcome lives only in the small status line at the very bottom of the screen. There is no legible on-table end-of-hand presentation.

## 3. Verdict-validity statement (Muse, on Muse's own verdicts)
The `4423a07` PASS — and the `330863f` / `958e7c0` verdicts before it — measured: seat/card overlap (the P1 that failed 443a27f), layout stability across chrome/orientation changes, gameplay-UI correctness, privacy (server diff), and full-suite greenness. **Those findings stand; nothing in this audit overturns them.**

What the verdicts did NOT measure: (a) felt proportions against any owner-meaningful standard — the suite's 0.95–1.42 portrait band was accepted as given, and 1.19 passes it while failing the owner's eye; (b) unused-viewport share; (c) winner-line legibility (the clamp guarantees clipping, and no test reads the message element's scroll height vs client height); (d) overlay obstruction area over the hero seat; (e) end-of-hand presentation quality. **The PASS was valid for its criteria, and its criteria were incomplete for the product question "does this table look and read like a premium poker table on a phone." That gap is Muse's** — the acceptance criteria AC1–AC5 that Muse wrote after the 443a27f failure were collision criteria, and they were met; nobody's criteria, Muse's included, covered proportions, emptiness, or legibility until the owner's screenshot supplied them.

## 4. Proposed measurable acceptance criteria for Claude's new table candidate (Issue #4)
To be applied by Muse's audit of the exact candidate SHA, alongside all standing criteria (zero intra-seat overlap; stability; suites green; server diff discipline):
- **G1 Portrait felt aspect:** h/w ≤ 1.05 at 390×844 and 375×812 for 6–9-handed tables (a near-round oval), unless the owner approves a different target number after seeing screenshots. No "tall" class above 1.10 in portrait.
- **G2 Viewport use:** below-felt share ≤ 22% of viewport height mid-hand at 390×844; largest unassigned empty band inside the table area ≤ 12 px; the felt+seats composition must visually anchor the dock (no floating table).
- **G3 Winner legibility:** the result message renders fully — element scrollHeight ≤ clientHeight for the longest realistic strings (two names + split + amount + hand category) at 320–430 px widths; no line-clamp below the space the design gives it.
- **G4 Overlay discipline:** coach/reset overlays cover 0 px² of the hero's cards and ≤ 2% of the table area over the hero seat, are dismissible, and auto-expire; measured with the tip visible.
- **G5 End-of-hand state:** at the result, the table itself shows winner + amount + hand legibly for the full result pause (pot area transitions to the result; the outcome does not exist only in the bottom status line).
- **G6 Landscape unchanged:** aspect ≤ 0.8, no empty band > 20 px, all standing criteria hold.
- **Evidence form:** before/after screenshots at 390×844 + 844×390 (9-handed mid-hand, showdown result, coach tip visible) filed with the candidate, plus the geometry numbers above — screenshots alone are not evidence, numbers alone are not either.
