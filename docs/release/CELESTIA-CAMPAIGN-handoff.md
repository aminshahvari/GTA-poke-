## What is built
1. **Campaign links, ported from `public-onboarding` `8b2b1fa` (Muse PASS `91bfd7f`) onto R2.**
   - Owner only, from Director → Public links.
   - Each link has its own source label, cap (1–2000) and expiry (up to 30 days).
   - Joiners see a welcome and consent page first and are **never** signed up for the open tournament.
   - At most 5 new accounts per connection per hour.
   - Responses are generic when a link is full, expired or cancelled.
   - The port's narrower inviter filter was dropped in favour of invite-privacy: no member receives `invitedBy` for anyone.
2. **`/v/<slug>`**, per `docs/vision/V-REDIRECT-DESIGN.md`.
   - The owner points `/v/celestia` at an open campaign link whose source is `celestia` ("Use for /v/celestia"). It can be re-pointed or cleared at any time, so the printed address never changes.
   - The response is a `302` to `/join/` plus the stored 12-character code, and nothing from the request is copied into it.
   - Every failure gives one byte-identical neutral page, "This invitation is not active right now.": unknown slug, bad characters, no mapping, link full, expired or cancelled.
   - `Cache-Control: no-store`, `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`, `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`, no cookie.
   - 60 requests per minute per connection, then `429`.
3. **The landing page**: the existing navy and gold sign-in screen with a campaign welcome.
   - The welcome has the venue line "You were invited at Celestia Restaurant & Lounge.", "Play. Connect. Belong. Grow." and "Together We Rise."
   - The consent notice shown before joining covers: free, no real money; 19+; what members see; membership is visible; contact details private; the nickname advice.
   - There are no partnership, offer, discount, prize, reward or attendance claims, and the rules link is labelled "club rules".
   - The venue line comes from a fixed server table, `VENUES`, keyed by source; other sources show no venue line.
4. **The optional email.**
   - Its own unticked box, with the wording "Blindrise may email me about club events. I can ask the club owner to remove my email at any time."
   - An email without the box ticked is refused, and a malformed email is refused.
   - It is stored in a separate `contact:<uid>` record: the email, plus `consent: { v, text, at }` with the exact wording shown.
   - Only the owner sees it, in the Director campaign report. It is never in any member payload.
   - Nothing sends email, and PIN and sign-in are unchanged.
5. **Measurement, owner-only, aggregate.** Per source:
   - **visits:** `/v/` scans of a mapped path;
   - **welcome views:** open campaign link pages shown;
   - **joined;**
   - **in a tournament:** registered in the current tournament or in any finished tournament's results;
   - **came back:** signed in again more than 20 hours and at most 30 days after joining, counted once per person.
   
   Counters are plain integers in `cstat:<source>`, with no address, time, device or person; "came back" is one flag on the member record. Empty states show 0, and nothing is estimated.
6. **Off switch.** Everything above is active only with `CAMPAIGNS=1`, and production does not set it. (Superseded 19 Oct: `STAGING=1` no longer switches campaigns on, and campaign consent now fails closed; see `CELESTIA-REMEDIATION-handoff.md`.) With campaigns off:
   - `/api/campaign`, `/api/vmap` and `/api/campaignInfo` answer exactly like an unknown endpoint (`404 {"error":"Not found"}`);
   - `/v/...` is an unknown path (404, no redirect);
   - a campaign invite left in the data cannot be redeemed;
   - no payload carries campaign data.

## Campaign assets
In `/mnt/project-files/blindrise/celestia/`, with copies in `docs/celestia/`:
- `celestia-a5-card.pdf`: A5 portrait with 3 mm bleed. The page is 437 × 612 pt (154.2 × 215.9 mm); trim 3 mm on every side to 148 × 210 mm. Black and gold, with Cormorant Garamond and IBM Plex embedded, and **"DRAFT — NOT FOR DISTRIBUTION"** bands top and bottom.
- `celestia-a5-card-preview.png`: a 300 dpi preview.
- `celestia-qr.svg`: the QR on its own.
- The QR is generated by **reportlab's QR encoder** (`reportlab.graphics.barcode.qrencoder`, already installed; no install was needed for it).
  - Version 4, error correction **Q**, 4-module quiet zone, **38.0 mm** printed.
  - It encodes only `https://gta-poker.onrender.com/v/celestia`, which is printed in words under it.
  - Source scripts: `qr_svg.py` (QR) and `build_card.js` (card). Set `FINAL=1` to drop the draft bands, and only after the owner authorizes it.
- Card copy:
  - The name Celestia does not appear on the card itself.
  - The fine print says "Blindrise is independent of this venue. No offers or benefits come with joining."
  - It also says free, 19+, no real money, no betting, and that points have no cash value.
- **QR verification** (`verify_qr.py`, output in `qr-verification.txt`).
  - It uses an independent decoder, OpenCV's `QRCodeDetector` (`opencv-python-headless`, installed once with pip for this check).
  - It asserts an exact match. **6 of 6 decoded exactly**:
    - the PDF rendered at 300 dpi as printed;
    - the PNG preview;
    - the whole card scaled to 1080, 720 and 540 px wide;
    - a simulated phone photo (tilted, blurred, darker, JPEG quality 70).
  - A real phone camera on a printed proof is still owed (owner, checklist step 5).
- **The route does not exist on production yet.** Scanning today gives production's 404 until this branch is deployed with `CAMPAIGNS=1` and the owner maps `/v/celestia`. That is why the card stays a draft.

## Rollback
- Unset `CAMPAIGNS`: everything goes dark (proven by `t_campaign_off`) and data stays.
- Or deploy the previous release. The new keys (`vpath:`, `cstat:`, `contact:`, the `campaign` and `campRet` fields on users) are additive and ignored by older code. No migration in either direction.
- To delete opted-in emails: remove the `contact:` records (owner request; not built as a button).
