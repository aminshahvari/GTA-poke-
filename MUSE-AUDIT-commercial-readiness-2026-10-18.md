## 3. Risk audit
- **Privacy (live risk if clubs are open).** Members are identified by real names; business profiles are member-visible by design. The consent/visibility copy that explains this (campaign criteria C3) exists **only in the undeployed campaign build** — members who joined through group links were auto-enrolled without that notice. F1 contact protection is enforced by the deployed server at write time, which bounds the leak risk for anything created since; the fresh-export content audit (Muse's run on the Oct 17 00:41 export found **zero** club records — nothing pre-dating the rules) must be re-run on current data now that the clubs may be open.
- **Authentication.** Name + short PIN is weak for any public-facing growth: the member list is enumerable by design inside the club, and there is no recovery channel. Acceptable for a small invite-only group with rate limits; not acceptable at public scale without the campaign build's mitigations and a recovery story.
- **Mobile stability.** The confirmed iPhone viewport defect (table geometry moves 1:1 with browser-chrome height changes; measured by Muse on the deployed code and on the hotfix tree) is the single largest user-facing risk. The fix has still not been delivered by Claude. Any growth or marketing push to an iPhone-heavy audience before this fix ships and is device-verified is a NO-GO.
- **Onboarding.** Current production: invite link → name + PIN + 19+/rules agreement → auto-enrolment into an open tournament. No contact capture means no re-engagement, no announcements channel, no account recovery. The campaign build (Muse PASS, C1–C7, `8b2b1fa`) fixes consent and expectations but is undeployed and would need a combined-candidate delta audit against the R2 line.
- **Multi-club tenant isolation.** **Not a capability.** There is no tenancy boundary in the data model to isolate: one deployment = one community. Any strategy that assumes selling or licensing Blindrise to multiple clubs is describing an unbuilt product (tenancy refactor or per-club deployments, plus payments, support and legal), and any isolation claim made today is unsupported.
- **Commercialization.** No payment rails, no pricing, no terms/privacy surface on record, and the Ontario flags from the Founder Vision audit (gaming model, PIPEDA, CASL, AODA, age policy) remain open for legal review; investment functionality stays **blocked** pending qualified Ontario counsel. The expiring Postgres plan (Nov 14) is a hard operational deadline under any commercial timeline.
