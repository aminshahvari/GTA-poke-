## Area 6 — Gameplay proposals (Show Cards / hand-to-hand delay / Rabbit Hunt): NOT DELIVERED — no verdict possible
A search of every branch finds no implementation and no proposal document for any of the three (the only mentions anywhere are in Muse's own overnight report). Fairness evaluation, recorded now so any future delivery is judged against it:
- **Sealed-deck baseline (verified in the deployed code, `server/fair.js`):** each tournament has a secret server seed whose SHA-256 seal is public before the first hand; every hand's deck is derived as SHA-256(seed | public drand beacon | table | hand) and saved for post-hoc verification. Any feature that reveals cards must preserve this chain.
- **Rabbit Hunt:** acceptable only if it reveals the next cards **of the actual committed deck** for that hand (sliced from the dealt deck after the hand fully ends). Re-drawing or re-shuffling breaks provable fairness = **automatic FAIL**. Must be off by default in tournament play or explicitly house-ruled.
- **Show Cards:** acceptable only strictly opt-in by the card holder, through the existing `shown` snapshot mechanism (hole cards are otherwise masked — verified), and never while decisions remain in the hand (a shown fold leaks dead-card information to players still acting).
- **Hand-to-hand delay:** hand-for-hand synchronization already exists in the engine (`hfhState`). A separate pace delay is acceptable only if server-driven, uniform across tables, and neutral to the level clock and action timers.
